# Paper reading protocol

Read in the order: scientific question → modeling assumptions → observation equation → optimization/inference objective → derivation → algorithm → experimental protocol → evidence. Distinguish physical model from computational approximation. For each central equation, identify variable dimensions, units, what is observed, what is latent, and how its parameters are estimated. Translate supplied English excerpts sentence by sentence when requested; do not fabricate verbatim passages. Explain each paragraph before jumping to unrelated innovation suggestions. Ask the researcher to reconstruct why the method follows from the problem, not merely paraphrase it. At 3–5 paragraphs, pause for three comprehension/transfer questions. End with a thinking card, not a generic summary.
