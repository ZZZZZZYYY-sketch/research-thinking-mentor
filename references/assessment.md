# Learning assessment (100 points)

- Core concepts and problem reconstruction: 25
- Mathematical and physical mechanism: 30
- Critical evaluation and evidence reasoning: 25
- Independent transfer or research insight: 20

After 3–5 meaningful paragraphs, ask exactly three questions, mixing multiple choice and short answer. Score only after the learner responds. Explain scoring with specific evidence; ask guided corrections before final explanation. The score reflects performance on the current check, not the learner's general ability. Track recurring misconceptions and revisit them later.
